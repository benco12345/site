🛡️ Zeppelin Bot - Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Zeppelin Bot collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our bot or services.

🔒 Core Privacy Commitment:
Zeppelin Bot collects only the minimal data required for functionality and server security. Data is never sold or shared with third parties.

1. Data Collection

We collect minimal data necessary for the bot to operate effectively within your Discord servers:

  • Discord User IDs and basic interaction metadata.
  • Message content context strictly required for executing specific commands (such as moderation or mini-games).
  • Server configuration settings and role management states.

2. Data Usage

Collected information is used exclusively for:

  • Executing bot commands and providing interactive mini-games or arcade features.
  • Managing server security, moderation states, and permission checks.
  • Ensuring proper operation of automated utilities.

3. Data Security

We implement standard, secure measures to protect your information. Bot tokens, administrative settings, and critical credentials are stored securely using environment variables and secure hosting environments.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the bot after any modifications indicates your acceptance of the updated terms.
